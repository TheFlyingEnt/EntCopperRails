<b>Addings Speed Controlled Copper Rails mimicing the "Experimental Minecart Features"</b>



## What is Adds ![copper-line-ui](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/8b37ce388c846c7a63480048afca7066998ce976.png)
- Copper Rails - A new rail that sets a minecart's speed limit, in four oxidation tiers (each with a waxed variant).
- Per-cart speed control - every minecart remembers its own top speed, from 8 up to 64 blocks/second.
- Fast without flying off - carts stay glued to the track even at full tilt, so high speed finally feels good.

## Control Your Minecart's Speed ![copper-line-ui](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/8b37ce388c846c7a63480048afca7066998ce976.png)

Every minecart now tracks its own max speed. A freshly placed cart starts at 8 BPS (blocks per second) and can be pushed all the way up to 64 BPS.

Copper Rails change that number as a cart rolls over them:
- Powered copper rail: raises the cart's max speed
- Unpowered copper rail: lowers it

How much each rail moves it depends on the copper's oxidation level; unoxidized copper makes bigger changes:

![copper-rail-speed](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/e3911e67ccae1279add85a0579745168e5de09fd.png)
> Powered rails are still needed to actually accelerate! Copper Rails set the speed limit, regular powered rails do the pushing
